how to make well

- strong causal relationship - two modes complement and strongly affect each other (shared resources, consequences)
- modes are complementary - some problems can't be solved by both modes
- modes are substantially different - different verbs
- both need to be fun

some pitfalls
- too tightly coupled - doing too well in one mode overwhelms the other mode (huge armies in total war making battles unbalanced)
- loosely coupled - strategy layer doesn't really affect tactical layer, just slightly different battle map, repetition

autoresolve as escape valve to skip boring battles, imperfect solution, hard to balance (autoresolve too good)

how does modern total war improve coupling
- terrain determines tactical battle - attacking across a river turns to a river fording battle etc
- army stance - force march, ambush, fortify etc (strategy layer verbs), modify how the battle starts
- reinforcement direction, affected by strategic map
- army state - injured, fatigued, experience - carries over to tactical layer

proposal: mission dependent on strategic situation
examples
- VIP/object/civilian extraction, search and destroy/hack
- reach escape point
- survive time period / waves

taxonomy
- prevent escape (tower defense-like)
- capture position
- hold out for X time
- infiltration/sabotage
- escape with X% of forces
